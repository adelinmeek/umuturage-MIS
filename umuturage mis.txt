**Use Case Diagram Specifications**

* **System Name:** Umuturage MIS
* **Primary Objective:** Streamline citizen relocation, registration, and management across local administrative levels in Rwanda.

---

### **Actors**

* **Citizen:** Registers/logs in, submits relocation/deregistration requests, manages family member details, views history on the dashboard, and accesses local leaders' contact details.
* **Cell Officer:** Reviews, verifies, and approves or rejects citizen registration/relocation requests within their cell.
* **Sector Officer:** Oversees cell-level operations, views aggregated relocation reports, and manages administrative escalations.
* **District Officer:** Monitors district-wide relocation trends, manages sector-level administrative data, and views high-level reporting.
* **Admin:** Manages system user roles, configures administrative units, oversees system logs, and maintains security settings.

---

### **Use Cases Grouped by Module**

#### **1. Authentication & Account Management**

* **Create Account:** Citizens register using personal identifiers (Full Name, National ID, Phone Number).
* **Login / Logout:** Authenticates all actors (Citizens, Officers, Admin) with role-based access.
* **Manage Profile:** Citizens update their personal details and add/edit family members.

#### **2. Relocation & Registration Module**

* **Submit Relocation Request:** Citizen initiates a registration request for a new location or a deregistration from a current location.
* **Attach Family Members:** Citizen links dependent details to the relocation application.
* **Review & Approve Registration:** Cell Officer verifies the submitted IDs and details, approving or rejecting the request.

#### **3. Information & Dashboard Module**

* **View Personal Dashboard:** Citizen tracks application history, status (Pending/Approved), and relocation logs.
* **View Leadership Information:** Displays contact and profile details of assigned local leaders upon successful registration.
* **View Administrative Analytics:** Sector and District Officers access population movement and relocation statistics.

#### **4. System Administration**

* **Manage System Users:** Admin creates, updates, and assigns administrative roles for Cell, Sector, and District Officers.
* **Audit & System Logs:** Admin monitors overall system operations and system audit trails.

---

* *Attach Family Members* `<<extend>>` *Submit Relocation Request* (optional step based on household status)
* *Reject Application* `<<extend>>` *Review & Approve Registration* (executed if details fail validation)
